What kind of game is Rune Ricochet?

Rune Ricochet is a single-player, turn-based brick breaker roguelite. It combines bouncing-ball combat with dungeon strategy: aim your orbs through enemies and wards, choose upgrades between rooms, and face bosses as you progress.

How do you play?

Drag to aim, then release to fire a volley of enchanted orbs. Use wall bounces to reach targets and chain hits. Read enemy intents before your next shot, then choose your rewards and the next encounter between rooms. The game uses portrait controls.

Is Rune Ricochet available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Rune Ricochet is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Is Rune Ricochet free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play with ads and an optional premium purchase. For purchase details and support, see the Terms of Service.

How do power-ups and fusion work?

Choose upgrades as you clear rooms, add orbs, and strengthen abilities through tiers. Compatible powers can fuse into new effects. For example, fire and ice combine into Thermal Shock: watch the fusion reveal and volley.
